Normalization in DBMS

Normalization is the process of organizing data to minimize redundancy and dependency.

  • Definition: Systematic decomposition of tables
  • Types: 1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF
  • Example: Student → Course mapping
  • Advantages: Reduces anomalies, ensures consistency
